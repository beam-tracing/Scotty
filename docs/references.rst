References
==========

The beam model that Scotty uses:

- V. H. Hall-Chen, F. I. Parra and J. C. Hillesheim, "Beam model of Doppler
  backscattering", *Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion* **64**, 095002
  (2022), https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6587/ac57a1

Cited in the code:

- T. H. Stix, *Waves in Plasmas* (American Institute of Physics, 1992): the
  cold-plasma dielectric tensor and the Booker quartic
  (`scotty.fun_general.find_Booker_discriminant`)
- P. F. Goldsmith, *Quasioptical Systems* (IEEE Press, 1998): the Gaussian
  beams of horns (`scotty.antenna.horns`)
- E. Stefanikova et al. (2016), https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4961554: the
  pedestal fit (`scotty.profile_fit.StefanikovaFit`)
